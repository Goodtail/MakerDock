# MakerDock website

The page introduces a local 3MF library and print planner for Mac. Its central problem is concrete: repeated downloads, filenames with little context, and no record of what was printed.

White and neutral charcoal surfaces share the native app's cobalt accent. Self-hosted Manrope handles Latin headings; native system fonts handle reading and CJK. Motion is limited to controls and respects reduced-motion preferences. No navy backgrounds, decorative gradients, stock testimonials, or invented usage figures.

The split hero puts the Mac download beside the product description, followed by a large real library capture. The problem section uses numbered editorial rows. An accessible feature tour shows plates, print records, batch organization, and the print queue. All feature descriptions exist in the server-rendered HTML. The browser section explains in-app MakerWorld tabs and download reuse. Local storage, practical FAQs, and a compact download block finish the page.

Each of the four languages has matching app screenshots, metadata, social cards, and a getting-started guide. English is served at `/`; explicit `/en` URLs redirect to the canonical English pages. Screenshots use original demo models and illustrative estimates. They show real app screens, not the user's library. Social cards compose those captures without changing their contents.

Copy describes the public 0.1.2 build. Local printer status is optional and device-dependent, completion is confirmed by the user, Fusion receives STL geometry, and the Chrome companion is planned. Experimental network/profile capture is not advertised as available. Tab continuity applies while the app is running, not full cross-relaunch restoration.
