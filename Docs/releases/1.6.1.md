# MakerDock 1.6.1

Historical release note: this version number was assigned prematurely. Public releases now start at [0.1.0](0.1.0.md); the earlier source and checkpoints are preserved.

MakerWorld is back in the sidebar. Browse the site, open a saved model's original page or print profile, and visit **My Collections** without leaving MakerDock.

- **MakerWorld** opens the site in the app's browser.
- **My Collections** follows the account signed in on MakerWorld. If you have not signed in within the app, sign in on the website and choose the shortcut again.
- Saved model and print-profile links open in the same browser.
- The navigation and sign-in guidance are available in English, Korean, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese.

Your existing library, notes, categories, and print records stay in place when updating.

## Download

Requires **macOS 13 or later**, on **Apple Silicon or Intel**. Download `MakerDock-1.6.1-universal.dmg`, open it, and drag MakerDock into Applications.

The app and installer are signed with **Developer ID Application: CHANWOO KOO (D523TSBMWR)**, accepted by Apple's notarization service, and stapled. The app inside the final installer passes Gatekeeper. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the final installer checksum.

## Integration

Ordinary site downloads use a save dialog, and Studio links open the separately installed official Bambu Studio. Automatic download capture and profile metadata extraction remain disabled in the public release. The Chrome companion extension is planned.

WebKit keeps your MakerWorld sign-in session locally. The collections shortcut reads the account navigation link shown by the website when requested; it does not copy credentials or use private account APIs. See [Privacy](../../PRIVACY.md) and [integration status](../integration-status.md).

Validation: 10 selected development tests and 5 public-build tests passed, covering navigation, collection-link resolution, delayed page content, abandoned requests, language catalogs, and the automatic-capture boundary. The installed production app was also checked against the live MakerWorld home and signed-in collections pages.
